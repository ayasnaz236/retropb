Camera Setup

For a Sony camera (e.g. A6000), use digiCamControl (free, Windows) to tether the camera over USB. Configure it to auto-save each capture into the folder below. This app will pick up new photos automatically.

digiCamControl itself must be running (it launches automatically in the background and minimizes itself) with the camera connected for the Take Photo button to trigger the shutter.

Exposure / white balance / focus are applied only if your camera and OS driver expose them.

Printer

Only enable this if your printer/paper combination actually supports true borderless printing. On some printers (e.g. certain Epson photo printers with custom paper sizes), forcing this can cause the printer to fill the uncovered area with black instead of leaving it blank. If prints come out wrong, turn this off first.

WiFi Photo Sharing

Guests scan a QR code after their photo to download it over your venue's WiFi or the laptop's own hotspot — no internet or app install required.

Booth Presets

    Event Mode

    An event bundles a preset (template, layout, printer, camera) under a name. Starting an event loads that preset and switches the booth into Operator Mode — a simplified screen with settings hidden.
